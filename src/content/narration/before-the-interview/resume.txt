One page, if you have under ten years of experience. A screener spends about ten seconds on it. Restricting the page to your most impressive material guarantees they see it, and adding more only competes with it. A long resume is not evidence of more experience. It is evidence of not prioritising.

Include only the roles that make you stronger, not a complete employment history. Then write every bullet the same way: accomplished X, by doing Y, producing measurable result Z. Not worked on the search feature, but cut search latency from nine hundred milliseconds to a hundred and twenty by rebuilding search on an inverted index, lifting search led purchases eighteen percent.

Then cut anything you cannot defend. Most interviewers treat everything on the page as fair game, and a line you cannot discuss for five minutes is worse than no line at all.

For languages, list most of what you have used with an honest level attached: expert, proficient, prior experience. Plain English beats years of experience, which is unreadable. And skip office software, age, marital status and nationality, and get someone to proofread it, because some screeners discard on a typo.
